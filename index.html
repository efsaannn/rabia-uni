Important Guidelines

International Students

Mandatory FRRO registration must be completed within 14 days of arrival in India. Please submit your passport and visa copies to the Admin Block.

Zero Tolerance Policy

The campus maintains a strict anti-ragging and non-discrimination policy to ensure a safe environment for everyone.

Green Campus

Rabia University is a plastic-free zone. Students are encouraged to use designated recycling points across the 100-acre site.

Culture, History & Nature

Our university is just a step away from one of the world's richest cultural heritage zones, Agra and its surroundings.

World Heritage

Taj Mahal, Agra

This architectural masterpiece near our campus is a living part of history and art studies for our students.

Historical Site

Bateshwar Dham

A complex of 101 ancient temples on the banks of the Yamuna. Discover the spiritual atmosphere of the region.

Wild Life

Etawah Lion Safari

The region's largest wildlife conservation park where you can see Asian lions in their natural habitat.

Local Culture

Balaji Shakti Peeth

The local spiritual hub of Shikohabad. An iconic structure where locals and students find peace.
